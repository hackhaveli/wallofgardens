Master Brand Identity System

WALL OF GARDENS

A true world-class logo is not decoration. It is a single, inescapable visual idea.
Here are 3 masterstroke identities combining Architectural Engineering with Living Nature.

DIRECTION 01 The Negative-Space Living Wall

The "Aha!" Discovery (Negative Space): Look closely at the 4 golden architectural columns that form the engineered 'Wall'. The negative space cut out between them reveals a graceful, ascending botanical leaf curling upwards towards the light. Solid = The Engineered Wall. Void = The Living Garden.

DIRECTION 02 The Interlocking 'W + G' Monogram

Haute Couture Architecture (Interlocking Monogram): Like the world's most revered luxury houses (Cartier, Yves Saint Laurent, Audemars Piguet), this creates a timeless monogram where the structured architectural 'W' (Wall) seamlessly weaves with the flowing biophilic loop of 'G' (Garden).
Versatility Test:
Stamp / Wax Seal Compatible
Embroidered Uniform Monogram
Watermark on Architectural Blueprints

DIRECTION 03 The Isometric Living Facade (3D Spatial)

The Spatial Dimension (Isometric Perspective): A minimalist geometric isometric cube showing an architectural room. The left facet is rigid architectural slats; the top is open skylight space; and the right facet bursts with living green flora. This literally depicts a Wall transformed into a Garden.
